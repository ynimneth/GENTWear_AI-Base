# GENTWear Store Policies

This document lists the official customer service policies of GENTWear, including return, exchange, shipping, payment, refund, and warranty guidelines.

## Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your GENTWear garments. If you are not satisfied, you may return or exchange your items.

*   **Policy Window:** Returns and exchanges are accepted within **30 days** from the date of delivery.
*   **Item Condition:** Items must be in their original condition: unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and with all original tags and labels attached. Customized or hemmed trousers are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.
*   **Return Process:**
    1. Log in to your GENTWear account and navigate to the Orders section.
    2. Select the order and click "Request Return/Exchange".
    3. Print the prepaid shipping label generated by our system.
    4. Package the items securely and drop them off at any authorized carrier location.
*   **Return Shipping Cost:** Return shipping is **free for GENTWear Loyalty Members**. Non-members will have a flat rate return processing fee of **$7.50** deducted from their refund.

---

## Shipping & Delivery

We offer fast, reliable shipping options to get your premium wardrobe to your doorstep:

*   **Standard Shipping:**
    *   *Delivery Time:* **3 to 5 business days**.
    *   *Cost:* **Free** for all orders over **$150**. For orders under $150, a flat rate of **$9.95** applies.
*   **Express Shipping:**
    *   *Delivery Time:* **1 to 2 business days**.
    *   *Cost:* Flat fee of **$24.95** for all orders.
*   **International Shipping:**
    *   We deliver to select countries worldwide. Delivery times range from 7 to 14 business days. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout.

*Note: Orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed before 1:00 PM EST are shipped the same business day.*

---

## Refund & Payment Processing

*   **Secure Checkout:** All payments are processed through **Stripe**, ensuring industry-leading encryption and security for your transactions.
*   **Accepted Payment Methods:** We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover) and digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay).
*   **Refund Timeline:** Once a return is received and inspected at our warehouse, your refund will be processed. Refunds are issued to the original payment method (Stripe card or wallet) within **5 to 7 business days**. An email notification will be sent once the refund is finalized.

---

## Manufacturing Warranty

At GENTWear, we stand behind the premium quality of our products.
*   **Warranty Period:** All garments are covered under a **1-year manufacturing defect warranty** from the date of purchase.
*   **What is Covered:** Stitching failures, loose seams, defective zippers, missing buttons upon arrival, or premature fabric deterioration.
*   **What is Not Covered:** General wear and tear, damage caused by improper dry cleaning or washing, shrinkage, or alterations made by third-party tailors.
*   **To File a Claim:** Contact our customer concierge at `support@gentwear.com` with your order number and photos of the defect.

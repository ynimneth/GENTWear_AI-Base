# GENTWear Premium Product Guide

Welcome to the GENTWear product catalog guide. This document serves as the ground truth for our product ranges, size selections, material selections, and styling tips.

## Product Categories

### 1. Suits
Our signature tailored suits represent the pinnacle of GENTWear craftsmanship. Available in classic, slim, and modern fits.
*   **The Westminster Suit:** A timeless British-style double-breasted suit, structured shoulders, double vents. Available in navy charcoal, and pinstripe wool.
*   **The Milano Suit:** Italian-inspired lightweight single-breasted suit, soft shoulders, slim-fit silhouette. Perfect for summer events or business-casual settings.

### 2. Blazers & Sport Coats
Versatile tailoring designed to bridge the gap between formal and casual.
*   **Classic Navy Blazer:** Double-button closure, gold or horn buttons, woven from Italian hopsack wool.
*   **Linen-Silk Sport Coat:** Unstructured and unlined, crafted from a breathable linen-silk-wool blend for premium summer styling.

### 3. Shirts
Premium dress and casual shirts tailored for durability and comfort.
*   **The Royal Oxford:** High-thread-count Egyptian cotton, classic spread collar, double-button French cuffs.
*   **Casual Button-Down:** Garment-washed linen or brushed twill cotton, button-down collar, single rounded cuff.

### 4. Trousers
Perfect drape and exceptional comfort in wool, cotton, and linen options.
*   **Tailored Wool Trousers:** Flat-front construction, half-lined, side adjusters instead of belt loops.
*   **Garment-Dyed Chinos:** Stretch cotton twill, casual fit, mid-rise, tapered leg.

---

## Fabric & Materials

We use only the finest responsibly sourced fabrics to construct our garments:
*   **Merino Wool (Super 120s - Super 150s):** Sourced from historic mills in Biella, Italy. Soft, breathable, naturally wrinkle-resistant.
*   **Egyptian Cotton (Giza 45):** Long-staple fibers providing exceptional strength, silkiness, and color retention.
*   **Belgian Linen:** Breathable and light, characteristically crisp drape with subtle slub texture.

## Styling Tips

*   **The Perfect Suit Drape:** Ensure the jacket sleeve ends 1/4 inch above the shirt cuff, showing a sliver of shirt linen.
*   **Color Harmony:** Navy blue suits look best with dark brown or burgundy oxford shoes. Grey suits are highly versatile and accommodate black, brown, or cognac footwear.
*   **Summer Dressing:** Pair the Linen-Silk Sport Coat with off-white Tailored Trousers and suede loafers (no socks) for an effortless Italian Riviera look.

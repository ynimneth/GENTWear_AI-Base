# GENTWear Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common questions about orders, payments, security, sizing, and account operations.

## Orders & Tracking

### How do I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, a shipping confirmation email containing a tracking number will be sent. You can also track your order status directly within your GENTWear account dashboard:
1. Log in to GENTWear.
2. Go to **My Profile** > **Order History**.
3. Select your order to view live tracking updates.

### Can I modify or cancel my order?
Because we process orders rapidly to ensure quick shipping, orders can only be changed or cancelled within **30 minutes** of placement. Please contact customer support immediately at `concierge@gentwear.com` or call us for urgent requests.

## Payments & Security

### Is my payment secure?
Yes, absolutely. We use **Stripe** for all checkout operations. GENTWear does not store your credit card details on our servers. Stripe is certified as a PCI Level 1 Service Provider, the most stringent level of security available in the payments industry.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept:
*   Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover credit/debit cards.
*   Apple Pay and Google Pay.
*   Stripe payment integration.

## Sizing & Fit Issues

### What if my garment doesn't fit?
We offer free exchanges within **30 days** of delivery. You can exchange your item for a different size or color. Simply initiate an exchange in your account order history, select the new size needed, and use the prepaid shipping label to send the original item back. We will dispatch the replacement item as soon as the carrier scans your return.

### Do you offer tailoring services?
Currently, we do not offer in-house tailoring or hemming. Our trousers come with standard inseam lengths (such as 32 inches) or unfinished hem options, allowing you to have them custom-tailored locally for a perfect fit.

## Account Management

### How do I reset my password?
If you have forgotten your password or want to update it:
1. Navigate to the **Login** page.
2. Click the **Forgot Password** link.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Check your inbox for a password reset link and follow the instructions to set a new password.

### How do I update my shipping or billing address?
To update your addresses:
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **My Profile** > **Addresses**.
3. You can edit existing addresses, delete them, or click "Add New Address" to save another one. You can also specify your default shipping and billing address.
